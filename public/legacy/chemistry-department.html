Welcome to Chemistry Department

The Department of Chemistry in St. Joseph’s Degree College was established in the Year 1994. Since its inception it is committed to prepare competitive and professional graduates within an innovative and intellectually stimulating environment. It supports other academic programs at St. Joseph’s Degree College by offering quality chemistry learning experiences.

The faculty in the Chemistry Department are qualified and experienced and are always student friendly.

The Chemistry Department prepares students for a diverse and changing world. It strives to contribute to a chemically literate society through teaching in class rooms, labs and research.  Fully established labs catering to various curricular requirements are available. Each lab is furnished with all the requirements as per the UGC norms. Students are given Individual kits to perform their experiments under the supervision of the trained faculty members.

Students are encouraged to experiment their ideas in the laboratory.Their experimental skills and potential to explore the real-life problems are always supported by the Department by planning number of extension activities and exhibitions. 

The Chemistry Department is committed to enhancing the public welfare and economic development of the College through 

Outreach programs
Participations and organizing symposium seminarsworkshops etc., and
Development of strategic partnerships with other departments, academic institutions and chemical industry.

Vision:

To explore new chemical facetsunderlying the living systems and physical processes
To encourage chemistry learning and research for greener environment which leads to a healthier living of mankind

Mission:

Inspire a knowledge platform that supports an inventive culture.
Educate future leaders about how chemistry underlies living systems and physical processes.
Build partnerships with the industry to achieve the department goals.
